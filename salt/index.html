About This System

The Loft's water supply comes from a private well. Before reaching any fixture, it passes through an iron filtration and water softening system that removes iron bacteria, sediment, and hard minerals. The softener works by running water through a resin bed that captures calcium and magnesium ions — salt is what regenerates that resin during periodic backwash cycles, flushing the captured minerals out and resetting the system.

When salt runs out, the resin can no longer regenerate. Hard, iron-laden water bypasses treatment and reaches the ice machine, glasswasher, coffee equipment, and taps — accelerating scale buildup, staining fixtures, and allowing iron bacteria to re-establish in lines that were previously clean. This monitor exists to catch depletion before that happens.
