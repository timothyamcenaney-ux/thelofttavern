Sensor mounted through lid, pointing down at the salt surface. The chart shows salt level, so it slopes down as the salt is used. Level % is worked out from the distance reading: 0% is salt about 27.5 in below the rim (just above the box in the bottom of the tank), 100% is the 6-bag fill line. Alert threshold: 15%.
